## Why this course

By the end of this journey you will be able to use **Docker** and **DevOps** practices on your own: package an application, make it run on any computer, publish it on a server and update it automatically.

Those words may mean nothing to you today. That is perfectly fine: this course **starts from zero**. You don't need to know how to program, and you don't need to have ever opened a terminal. You only need to know how to use a computer for everyday things: open a website, save a file, create a folder.

<Callout tipo="consiglio">
Every technical word is explained **before** it is used. If you find a word you don't understand and it hasn't been explained, that is the course's mistake, not yours.
</Callout>

## How it is organized

The course is divided into **worlds**. Each world is a stage of the journey: we start from the basics of computers and go all the way to real servers.

Each world contains a few **levels**. Each level contains:

1. Short **lessons**, 5-10 minutes of reading, like this one.
2. **Missions**: hands-on exercises that the site checks for you and gives feedback on.
3. At the end of the world, a **Boss Challenge**: a bigger exercise that brings together everything you have learned.

Levels unlock **in order**. It is not a rule meant to make your life harder: every level uses ideas explained in the previous ones.

<Analogia>
Think of a mountain trail divided into stages. Each hut (a level) can only be reached by passing through the previous one. Every now and then there is a harder pass (the Boss Challenge) that tells you whether you are ready for the next valley.
</Analogia>

## How to read a lesson

Technical lessons always follow the same pattern, so you always know where to find things:

- **Why you need it**: the real problem the topic solves.
- **A comparison**: an example from everyday life.
- **The concept**: the explanation, in plain words.
- **Example** and **line by line**: a real command or file, explained piece by piece.
- **Common errors**: the error messages you will see most often and what they mean.
- **In short**: a summary of a few points.

Technical words are **underlined with dots**. Tap them to open their definition. You will also find them all in the **Glossary**, in the top menu.

At the end of every lesson there is a **"Learn more in the official documentation"** box: links to the official pages the lesson was written from. Learning to read them is part of becoming independent.

## Your pace

There are no deadlines. You can do one lesson a day or a whole level in an afternoon. When you finish reading a lesson, tap the button at the bottom: the lesson is marked as read and you move on to the next one.

## In short

- The course starts from zero: every technical word is explained before it is used.
- It is divided into **worlds**, which contain **levels**, which contain **lessons** and **missions**.
- Levels unlock in order; every world ends with a **Boss Challenge**.
- Words underlined with dots can be tapped to read their definition.
