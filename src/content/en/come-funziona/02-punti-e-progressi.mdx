## Experience points (XP)

Everything you do in the course earns you **XP**, experience points:

- **10 XP** for each lesson read;
- usually **50 XP** for each mission passed;
- **200 XP** for each Boss Challenge.

XP make you climb **ranks**: you start as a **Deckhand** and can become a **Container Admiral**. Your rank and XP are always visible in the top right corner.

## Hints: asking for help is allowed

Every mission has up to **3 hints**: a clue, a stronger clue and finally the explained solution. Using them is allowed and useful, but each one reduces the mission's XP by 25%. Even if you use all the hints, part of the XP is still guaranteed.

<Callout tipo="consiglio">
Always try on your own first, even if you get it wrong. Mistakes don't cost points: there is even a badge for passing a mission after many attempts.
</Callout>

## Badges and streaks

**Badges** are special milestones: your first lesson, your first mission, a mission without hints, a week of study in a row. You will find them all on the **Profile** page, including the ones you haven't earned yet, with a note on how to earn them.

Your **streak** counts consecutive days of study. If you haven't studied today but you did yesterday, your streak is still alive: you have until tonight.

## Where your progress is saved

The program you are using to view this site is called a <Termine id="browser">browser</Termine>: Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge are all browsers.

The course **has no accounts and no server** keeping your data. Everything is saved in a small space that the browser reserves for each website: <Termine id="archiviazione-locale">local storage</Termine>.

<Analogia>
It is like a notebook left on your desk at home: it is handy and nobody else reads it, but if you move house (that is, change computer, phone or browser) the notebook does not come with you.
</Analogia>

This has three practical consequences:

1. If you open the course from **another device** or another browser, you will start from zero.
2. If you **clear your browser's browsing data**, your progress disappears.
3. In **private browsing** (or incognito mode) the browser deletes everything when you close it.

## Backup: export and import

From the **Profile** page you can **export** your progress: the site creates a file with all your data, which you can save wherever you like. On another device you use **import** and choose that file: you pick up exactly where you left off.

<Callout tipo="attenzione">
Importing a file **replaces** the progress stored in that browser. The site always asks for confirmation before doing it.
</Callout>

## In short

- Lessons, missions and Boss Challenges give **XP**; XP make you climb **ranks**.
- **Hints** help but reduce the mission's XP.
- Your progress lives in the browser's **local storage**: it stays on that device and in that browser.
- To switch device or keep a backup, use **export** and **import** on the Profile page.
